The MITRE ATT&CK mapping uses a hybrid approach: 15 deterministic rules map observed commands and protocol behaviour to technique IDs, supplemented by an LLM-generated narrative (Mistral 7B, temperature 0.3, max 80 tokens) that contextualizes the attack chain without introducing unverified technique IDs.3

Risk scoring model — 6 signals

SignalMax ptsDescription
IP Reputation25AbuseIPDB abuse confidence (thresholded). Skipped for private IPs.
Compromise Confirmed25Binary — successful auth or exploit activation on any honeypot.
Brute Force / Automation20Aggregate login volume and velocity. Bonus for targeted wordlists.
Malware Detected15YARA severity (CRITICAL / HIGH / match) + VT detection count.
Anonymization / Evasion10Tor / VPN / proxy usage + critical-port targeting (conditional).
Attack Sophistication5Kill-chain depth (0-8 stages) + multi-honeypot coordination bonus.
