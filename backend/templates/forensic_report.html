§ 00 General Introduction

In the context of proliferating cyberattacks, organisations face increasingly sophisticated threats. Early detection and rigorous forensic analysis are strategic imperatives for any SOC team.

This report is produced automatically by ForenHive, a forensic analysis platform based on a network of honeypots (Cowrie SSH, Dionaea, Galah HTTP, Zeek). ForenHive applies a criminological framework inspired by the FBI Behavioral Analysis Unit (BAU) — organised/disorganised concepts, Modus Operandi, Locard's exchange principle — to produce forensically valid and admissible attacker profiles.

This document covers the full analysis cycle of an incident detected via the {{ (session.get('honeypot') or 'unknown') | upper }} honeypot: log capture, multi-source IP enrichment, MITRE ATT&CK classification, malware analysis, LLM behavioural profiling (Mistral 7B local), deterministic scoring, and RFC 3161 certification.

Scoring Methodology
The risk score is computed deterministically from 6 independent signals: Reputation (AbuseIPDB abuse confidence, up to 25 pts), Compromise (successful authentication = confirmed breach, up to 25 pts), Brute Force (attempt volume and velocity, up to 20 pts), Malware (YARA match or VirusTotal hit, up to 15 pts), Anonymisation (Tor exit node or VPN/proxy detected, up to 10 pts), and Sophistication (multi-honeypot correlation, up to 5 pts). The total is capped at 100. No machine-learning model is involved — every point is traceable to a concrete log event or enrichment API response. Thresholds: CRITIQUE ≥ 80 · ÉLEVÉ ≥ 60 · MOYEN ≥ 40 · FAIBLE ≥ 20 · INFO < 20.
