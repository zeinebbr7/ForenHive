MITRE ATT&CK mapping uses a hybrid approach: deterministic rules map observed commands and protocol behavior to technique IDs (always correct, no hallucination), complemented by an LLM-generated narrative (Mistral 7B, temperature 0.1, max 80 tokens) that contextualizes the attack chain without introducing unverified technique IDs.3

Risk Scoring Model — 9 Signals (max 100)

Signals are grouped by FBI BAU criminological taxonomy — WHO (attacker identity), WHAT (intent & impact) and HOW (capability). Each signal is deterministic and evidence-based.
